The trust standard

One grading rubric, on every listing

A published, consistent standard is the anchor for buying refurbished with confidence. Every grade carries a written note and photos — acoustic performance is stated by rating, never implied.

Excellent

Near-new; minimal cosmetic wear; all functions as-new.

Good

Light cosmetic wear noted and photographed; fully functional.

Fair

Visible wear or minor repairs; priced to reflect it; fully functional.

Questions

Frequently asked

How much can I save buying a refurbished pod?

Around 50% versus the equivalent pod new. New one-person pods typically run £8,000–£20,000+; refurbished units on Podresale sell at roughly half, with the saving shown on every listing.

Which brands does Podresale list?

Every major brand on equal terms, including Framery, Silen, Hush, Rofa and HolmrisB8, from single-person phone booths to larger meeting pods.

Is every pod quality-checked?

Yes. Every pod is inspected and condition-graded — Excellent, Good or Fair — against a published rubric, with a written note, photos and warranty terms shown before you enquire.

Do you handle delivery and installation?

Delivery, dismantling and install are arranged through specialist logistics partners UK-wide, bundled into a single quote.

Can't find the pod I need listed?

Submit your requirements — brand, size, budget and location — and we'll alert you the moment matching stock is sourced and refurbished, often before it's publicly listed.

How do I sell a surplus pod?

List ex-lease, surplus or downsized stock in minutes and reach a nationwide pool of buyers. Consignment and buyback options are also available if you'd rather we handle the sale.

Does Podresale cover my part of the UK?

Podresale operates UK-wide, with the deepest stock and fastest logistics around London, Surrey and the Thames Valley, plus active coverage in Reading, Manchester, Birmingham, Bristol, Leeds and Edinburgh.
